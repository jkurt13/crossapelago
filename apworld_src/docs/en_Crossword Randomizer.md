# Crossword Randomizer

## What does randomization do to this game?
A crossword puzzle is generated from your seed. Choose the themes (General, Gaming, Movies, Sports),
the difficulty (easy, medium, hard) and the size (small to huge) in your options. Clues start hidden and you can only type letters you have
received. Other players send you **clues** ("3 Across Clue") and **letter keys** ("Letter R").

## What else can I receive?
- **Reveal Square**: fills in the square you pick.
- **Check Word**: marks the wrong letters in the current word.

Both are consumable. Your uses are saved on the server, so they follow you between devices.

## What is a check?
- Correctly solving a word whose clue you have unlocked.
- Optional milestones: solving 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of the words.
- Optional square checks (every square, or every Nth square). A square's check is sent when a word through it
  is solved or when you reveal it. Blue dots mark these squares in the client.

## What is the goal?
Solve every word in the puzzle.
