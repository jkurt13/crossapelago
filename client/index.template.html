Solve a crossword inside your Archipelago multiworld

Clues and letter keys are scattered across everyone's games. Solving words sends out checks.

  1. Download crossword_randomizer.apworld and put it in your Archipelago custom_worlds folder.
  2. Grab the YAML template, set your name and options, and generate as usual.
  3. Enter the server address and your slot name above, then press Connect.

Just want to try it? Press Demo for a small offline puzzle.

Tools are items other players can send you. Blue dots mark squares that are checks.
